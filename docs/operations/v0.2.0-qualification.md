## Migration and rollback

Follow the [migration guide](migration-guide.md) and
[release checklist](release-checklist.md). For each Midge 0.3 cloud deployment,
record its chosen logical migration or fresh-prefix procedure, preserved
original prefix/cache, restart recovery, local-cache-loss recovery, and rollback
rehearsal. Operator-specific rehearsals and acceptance remain outstanding.
Local deployments require a verified copy of `FITZ_STORAGE_PATH`.

Upgrade compatible Stream-error clients before the broker and roll back the
broker before those clients. RPC cancellation cannot undo a side effect, and a
canceled dispatched call is never automatically safe to retry. The planned
five-second cancellation grace period bounds broker session cleanup; it is not
proof that remote application effects were stopped or reversed.
